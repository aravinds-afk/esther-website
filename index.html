THE ORIGIN STORY

A ESTHER EVANJELINE, MA, MBA

Leadership isn't found in a textbook; it's forged in the fire of responsibility. As Executive Director of Chakra Critical Care Hospitals in Kolathur, Esther Evanjeline has spent two decades managing 101 ICU beds and 24/7 emergency services.

She has lived the crisis of every family walking through her doors. She knows that when people are at their most vulnerable, they don't need generic promises—they need action, competence, and a leader who refuses to back down.
