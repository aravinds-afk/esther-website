THE ORIGIN STORY

DR. A. ESTHER EVANJELINE, MA, MBA

Born and raised in Madurai, leadership isn't found in a textbook; it's forged in the fire of responsibility. As Executive Director of Chakra Critical Care Hospitals in Kolathur, Esther Evanjeline has spent two decades managing 101 ICU beds and 24/7 emergency services.

Under her leadership, Chakra Critical Care Hospitals expanded into a healthcare enterprise valued at nearly 200 Crores — reflecting her ability to build systems, scale institutions, and lead with a patient-first service philosophy rooted in ethical leadership, compassionate care, and accountability.

She has lived the crisis of every family walking through her doors. She knows that when people are at their most vulnerable, they don't need generic promises—they need action, competence, and a leader who refuses to back down.

Dr. A. Esther Evanjeline was honored with an Honorary Doctorate in Hospital Management by United American University, USA, recognizing her leadership excellence and contribution to healthcare administration.
